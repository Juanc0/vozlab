# Graph SVG and administration surfaces — recovery plan v1

Opened 2026-11-05. Branch `main` at `e0ee405`.

## Why this plan exists

After the branch consolidation the application appeared to have lost weeks of
work: the administration surfaces had no navbar entry, their pages rendered
without labels, the graph still used Cytoscape, and several test suites were
red. An evidence-based audit (`docs/audit/2026-11-05-historical-completeness.md`)
established that no work was deleted from history. Three separate defects
produce the symptom.

| Defect | What it is | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **D1 — the 2026-11-02 regression** | commit `2bf4b07` (cherry-pick of `e69431c`, "engines comparison tool and in-progress web changes") removed 1 012 lines across three files | `git show --stat 2bf4b07` |
| **D2 — the painter cannot build** | `d3-drag`, `d3-selection`, `d3-zoom`, `d3-force` are imported by `painter.ts` and `force-layout.ts` but appear in neither `apps/web/package.json` nor `node_modules` | `engines-svg-painter.test.ts` fails with 0 tests: *Failed to resolve import "d3-drag"* |
| **D3 — the surfaces are unlabelled** | the `admin`, `workspaceAdmin` and `ontologyAdmin` i18n namespaces went from 3 to 0 in `es.ts` | `grep -c` before `2bf4b07^`: 3, in the tree: 0 |

## Part II — D2, the painter stack

`SvgGraphCanvas.svelte` and `painter.ts` exist and are not mounted by anything.
They cannot be compiled as the tree stands:

| Blocker | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Missing dependencies | `d3-drag`, `d3-selection`, `d3-zoom`, `d3-force`; `apps/web/node_modules` holds only cytoscape, sigma and graphology |
| Missing i18n keys | `engines.svgPainterError` (used at `SvgGraphCanvas.svelte:147`), `graph.unreviewed` (`painter.ts:797,973`), and the six `explore.zoom*` keys that `ZoomControls.svelte` needs |
| Not a drop-in replacement | the painter has no `centerOn`, no `expandedEdgeIds`/relation cards, no `onpreview`, no `readableZoom`, no host-seeded `pinned`, and no dagre layout — every one of them is consumed by `GraphCanvas` or `ExplorationCanvas` call sites |

Parity, measured capability by capability: pan, zoom, fit, node drag, select,
edge select, expand badge, card chrome and destroy are at parity or better.
The painter **exceeds** the incumbent on double-click expand, zoom buttons,
idle sway and reduced-motion. It **lacks** four things the incumbent's call
sites depend on: dagre layout, `centerOn`, `expandedEdgeIds` (relation cards),
and the `readableZoom` clamp.

## Part VI — open decisions

**Decided 2026-11-05:** no dagre returns. The untangle action is a relaxed
force pass over the painter's own layout (`rearrange()` with a raised alpha),
not a layered arrangement.

Still open, both stated in plain terms because the wording of the audit leaked
its vocabulary into them:

1. **Hover — freeze or freeze plus a preview badge.** Two different behaviours
exist. The painter stops a node's idle sway while the pointer is over it
(`data-hovered`, `painter.ts:1051`), so the node holds still while it is read or
clicked — this is the behaviour the product owner described. The incumbent
additionally raised a floating HTML badge with extra node information on
`mouseover` (`instance.ts:341` → `onpreview`). *Question: keep only the freeze,
or keep the badge as well?*
2. **Relation cards — expand an edge into its own card.** A node expands into a
card; the incumbent could also expand an **edge**, drawing a card on the
relation with its data (`expandedEdgeIds` at `ExplorationCanvas.svelte:22,83`,
`ontoggleedge` at `:69`). The painter expands nodes only. *Question: is
expanding a relation still wanted, or is the inspector's relation reading
enough?* Without it, seeing a relation's detail costs two steps: select the
edge, then open the inspector.
